## What

CampusEats is a campus food-service system designed to make it easier for students and other campus users to discover and use available food services. The system provides a structured way to view food options, interact with food services, place requests or orders, and manage food-related activities.

The main purpose of CampusEats is to provide a convenient connection between people who want food and the food services available on or around the campus.

## Who

The main users and stakeholders of CampusEats are:

* **Students** — use the system to find food options and place food orders or requests.
* **Food-service providers** — provide food items and services through the system.
* **Campus administrators** — manage and oversee the food-service system.

## Summary

CampusEats connects campus users with food services through a single system. Students can discover food options and interact with food providers, while providers can manage their food offerings and orders. Administrators can oversee the overall system. The nouns identify the important entities and services, while the verbs describe the actions performed between them.
